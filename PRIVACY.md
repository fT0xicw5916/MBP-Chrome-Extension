# Privacy Policy for ManageBac Plus Extension (MBPE)

## Overview
ManageBac Plus Extension (MBPE) is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our extension collects, uses, and safeguards your information.

## Information Collection and Use
### What We Collect
- **Grade Information**: The extension reads grade data only from your ManageBac pages
- **Temporary Storage**: Grade calculations and simulation data are stored temporarily in your browser's local storage
- **No Personal Information**: We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information, login credentials, or identifiable data

### How We Use Information
- Grade data is used solely for GPA calculations and grade simulations
- All calculations are performed locally in your browser
- No data is transmitted to external servers or third parties

## Data Storage
- All data is stored locally in your browser using Chrome's storage API
- No data is stored permanently
- No cookies are used

## Third-Party Access
- We do not share any information with third parties
- We do not collect or transmit data to external servers
- The extension operates independently within your browser

## Required Permissions
The extension requires specific permissions to function:
- **tabs**: To detect ManageBac page navigation
- **scripting**: To read grade information from ManageBac pages
- **storage**: To temporarily store grade data for calculations
- **host_permissions** To operate only on ManageBac websites

## Data Security
- All operations are performed locally in your browser
- No external connections are made
- No data is transmitted outside your browser

## Updates
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the extension's documentation and the Chrome Web Store listing.
